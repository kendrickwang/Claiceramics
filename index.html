01 — Brand Foundation
Understanding clai.ceramics

Brand Voice

Warm, curious, and personal. Connie's multicultural story (Hong Kong → Nigeria → LA) and love of nature give the brand authentic depth.

Product Promise

Quirky, bold, functional art pieces that "spark joy" — each one unique, hand-sculpted, shaped by the clay itself.

Customer

Thoughtful buyers who value handmade, original art over mass-produced goods. Collectors, gift-givers, and home decorators.

Email Platform

Flodesk (already connected via "Where to Find Me" signup form) — ideal for visual, beautiful email layouts.

02 — Strategy Pillars
The 5 Content Pillars
🏺

The Making

Behind-the-scenes process content. Show the clay, the tools, the failures, and the magic of watching a piece come to life.

🌿

Nature & Inspiration

The natural world that fuels Connie's work — botanical forms, textures, living creatures. Educational, visual, story-driven.

✨

New Work & Drops

Product launches, restocks, and one-of-a-kind pieces. Create urgency and exclusivity for email subscribers first.

🗺️

Connie's World

Her story, her roots across three continents, her garden, her LA life. Humanize the maker — people buy from people.

🎁

Community & Occasions

Seasonal moments, gifting guides, markets and pop-ups, subscriber milestones. Celebrate the community around the work.

✦ Implementation Tips for Flodesk

→Set up the 3-email welcome sequence as a Flodesk Workflow triggered on list sign-up. Days 0, 3, and 7.
→Use Flodesk's visual editor to let photos of the work carry the email — keep text minimal and personal.
→Segment subscribers by purchase history to send post-purchase emails only to buyers, not the whole list.
→Link the Instagram/YouTube process videos in nurture emails — Flodesk embeds look beautiful on mobile.
→Use a consistent "From Name" — "Connie from clai.ceramics" — for human recognition and better open rates.
→Always send a test to yourself first and view on mobile — most of your audience will read there.
→Grow the list via the "Where to Find Me" Flodesk form — add to Instagram bio and market sign-up sheets.
→Run a quarterly audit: archive cold subscribers (90+ days no open) to keep deliverability high.
